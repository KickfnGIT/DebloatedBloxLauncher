Hello, I'm the creator of DBL.

DBL is a solo project built entirely by me. It originally started with AI-generated code, but after my subscription ended, I took the opportunity to learn Python and continue development myself.

I built DBL in response to issues with other Roblox bootstrappers. Many of them modify Roblox in ways that are getting users banned.

DBL is different. It's a fully debloated Roblox launcher that runs clean and undetected. It won't get you banned—unless you choose to use fast flags, which come with their own risks.

Thanks for checking out the project.
